# Hi, welcome to the ESA EarthCODE Hackathon 2026!

The [**EarthCODE Hackathon**](https://esa-earthcode.github.io/2026-earthcode-hackathon/) will take place from 30 November -- 4 December 2026 at [ESA ESRIN](https://www.esa.int/establishment/about-us/meet-us/visit-esa/esa-esrin), in Frascati, Italy.
The event will provide an opportunity to bring the communities who are using [EarthCODE](https://earthcode.esa.int/) tools and services as close as possible to the people who are building them.

The EarthCODE Hackathon will bring together [Ocean](https://eo4society.esa.int/communities/scientists/esa-ocean-science-cluster/), [Carbon](https://eo4society.esa.int/communities/scientists/esa-carbon-science-cluster/), and [Polar](https://eo4society.esa.int/communities/scientists/esa-polar-science-cluster/) scientists, experts in [FAIR](https://www.gofair.foundation/fair-principles) data and reproducible workflows and open source developers to focus on the creation and enhancement of analysis ready data collections.

## What you'll find here

This GitHub Organization is a place to collaborate on projects throughout the hackathon and discuss ideas.

- To share and explore hackathon project ideas, visit the [Project Ideas](https://github.com/esa-earthcode-hackathon/project-ideas) repository and post your idea as an issue, and/or browse [open issues](https://github.com/esa-earthcode-hackathon/project-ideas/issues) to see and chat about others' ideas.
- Use the [Project Template Repository](https://github.com/esa-earthcode-hackathon/earthcode_hackathon_proj_template) as a starting point to create a repository for your hackathon project.
